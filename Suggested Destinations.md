# Suggested Next Destinations

Based on where the group has already been — **Boston, New Orleans, Charleston, Austin, and Washington DC** — a pattern shows up:

- **Historic, walkable downtowns** (Charleston's squares, Boston's Freedom Trail, DC's Mall)
- **Strong food + music/nightlife scenes** (New Orleans jazz, Austin live music, Boston/DC food)
- **Mix of free and paid attractions** (DC's Smithsonians, Boston's historic sites vs. paid tours)
- **Moderate budget** — none of these are NYC/SF-tier expensive; mid-range hotels run ~$150–250/night, meals ~$15–40

Here are 8 destinations that match that profile, grouped by what they're most similar to.

## Top Picks

### 1. Savannah, GA — *like Charleston*
- **Why:** Same Southern historic-district charm, cobblestone squares, horse-drawn carriage tours, ghost tours.
- **Attractions:** Historic District, Forsyth Park, River Street, Bonaventure Cemetery.
- **Budget:** $ — noticeably cheaper than Charleston; hotels ~$120–180/night.

### 2. Nashville, TN — *like New Orleans + Austin*
- **Why:** Live music every night (honky-tonks instead of jazz clubs), huge food scene, walkable downtown.
- **Attractions:** Broadway honky-tonk strip, Country Music Hall of Fame, Ryman Auditorium.
- **Budget:** $$ — comparable to Austin; can get pricier on weekends.

### 3. San Antonio, TX — *like Austin + Charleston*
- **Why:** Texas culture and food like Austin, but with the historic-site density of Charleston (the Alamo, Spanish missions).
- **Attractions:** River Walk, The Alamo, historic missions (free, UNESCO site).
- **Budget:** $ — one of the more affordable options on this list.

### 4. Richmond, VA — *like DC + Boston*
- **Why:** Deep Revolutionary/Civil War history like DC, walkable riverfront, close enough for a long weekend.
- **Attractions:** American Civil War Museum, Belle Isle, VMFA (free museum).
- **Budget:** $ — cheaper hotels and food than DC, similar attraction quality.

### 5. Asheville, NC — *like Austin*
- **Why:** Same "weird, foodie, music-loving college-town energy" as Austin, plus mountains and breweries.
- **Attractions:** Blue Ridge Parkway, Biltmore Estate, River Arts District, brewery scene.
- **Budget:** $$ — food/breweries add up, but lodging is reasonable outside peak leaf season.

### 6. Philadelphia, PA — *like Boston + DC*
- **Why:** Revolutionary history like Boston, tons of free/cheap museums like DC, very walkable.
- **Attractions:** Independence Hall, Liberty Bell, Reading Terminal Market, art museum steps.
- **Budget:** $ — cheaper flights/hotels than Boston or DC for similar sightseeing.

### 7. Memphis, TN — *like New Orleans*
- **Why:** Music history (Beale Street, Sun Studio, Stax) and BBQ in place of Cajun food, same "soul of the city is the music" vibe.
- **Attractions:** Beale Street, Graceland, National Civil Rights Museum.
- **Budget:** $ — one of the cheapest trips on this list.

### 8. St. Augustine, FL — *like Charleston*
- **Why:** Oldest city in the US, historic Spanish architecture, coastal charm, smaller and cheaper than Charleston.
- **Attractions:** Castillo de San Marcos, historic downtown, nearby beaches.
- **Budget:** $ — budget-friendly, especially outside summer.

## Suggested Next Trip

If picking one: **Savannah, GA** is the closest match overall — it hits the historic-district charm of Charleston at a lower price point, and it's a short add-on trip from anywhere in the Southeast. For a livelier group trip, **Nashville** is the best New Orleans/Austin hybrid.
